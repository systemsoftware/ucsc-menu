# Privacy Policy for UCSC Menu

**Effective Date:** July 10, 2026

Thank you for using UCSC Menu! Your privacy is incredibly important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection and usage for the UCSC Menu iOS application.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.** 

UCSC Menu is designed to provide you with dining hall menus as simply and securely as possible. To achieve this:
* **No User Accounts:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details to use the app.
* **No Tracking or Analytics:** We do not use third-party analytics tools or advertising frameworks. We do not track your usage behavior within the app.
* **Local Storage:** Any app preferences you set (such as the custom order of your dining locations or dietary filters) are saved entirely locally on your device. We never sync or upload this data.

## 2. Network Requests and Third Parties

To provide you with the most up-to-date menus, UCSC Menu fetches data dynamically from the official UC Santa Cruz Dining FoodPro website. 
* **Direct Connections:** The app makes direct web requests from your device to the UCSC Dining servers and parses the information locally.
* **UCSC Server Logs:** Because the app interacts with the UCSC Dining website, their servers may collect standard internet log information (such as your IP address or user agent) in the same way they would if you visited their site using a standard web browser like Safari. We do not have access to, nor control over, this data. For information on how UC Santa Cruz handles web traffic data, please refer to their official privacy policies.

## 3. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Since our app does not collect personal data from any users whatsoever, it is safe for all ages.

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time if the functionality of the app changes. If any changes are made to how we handle data, this document will be updated, and the "Effective Date" at the top will be revised accordingly.
